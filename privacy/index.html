Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data Nightjar ("we", "us") processes when you visit this website or use the Nightjar service, why we process it, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Account data

Your email address, an optional name, a hashed password (we never store or see the plain password), your time zone, and your plan.

Monitoring data

The checks you create, and for every ping: its time, type, exit code, duration, the requesting IP address and user agent, and any request body you choose to send (stored as a log excerpt, truncated to your plan's limit). Please don't send secrets or personal data in ping bodies.

Alert channel settings

Webhook URLs, bot tokens, chat IDs, topics and email addresses you configure so we can deliver alerts, plus a log of each delivery attempt and the receiving service's response code.

Billing data

Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive and store a Stripe customer ID, subscription status, plan and renewal date. We do not receive or store card numbers.

Contact messages

If you use the contact form, we store your name, email, message and the IP address it was sent from (for abuse prevention).

Website

The marketing website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The application stores a session token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service: evaluate schedules, detect missed or failed runs, and send the alerts you configured.
  • To run your account and subscription, and to enforce plan limits.
  • To send service emails such as password resets and, where enabled, a welcome email. We don't send marketing email without your consent.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by rate limiting and investigating abuse.

The legal bases are performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interest in operating a secure service, and compliance with legal obligations (for example, tax records).

Alert destinations you configure (for example Slack, Discord, Telegram, Google Chat, ntfy or your own webhook) receive the alert content when an alert fires. Their own privacy policies apply.

Retention

Ping and event history is kept for your plan's retention window (7, 90 or 365 days) and deleted automatically. Delivery logs are kept for 30 days. Account data is kept until you delete your account. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can view and change your data in the app, and delete your account and all of its checks, history and channels from Settings at any time. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, port, restrict or object to processing. To exercise them, use the contact form.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with TLS. Database access is restricted with row-level security so each account can only read its own data. Ping URLs contain random tokens that you can rotate at any time.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, notify account holders.
